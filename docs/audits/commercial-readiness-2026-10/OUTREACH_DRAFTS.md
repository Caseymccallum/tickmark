“Tickmark gives a small practice one checklist per client and a link the client can open without an account. ## LinkedIn

Hi [Name] — I’m a local developer researching how small practices collect client paperwork and chase missing items. I’ve built an early checklist/link tool and want to understand where current systems work well or leave gaps. Would you be open to a short conversation? I’ll use synthetic data only.

## 20-minute call agenda

Ask for last difficult request, current system/frequency/cost, what “ready” means, client friction/mobile behaviour, security/procurement requirements, and what would make them switch or stop. Show a five-minute synthetic flow only if invited. Ask what price they would approve.

## Five-minute demo

Disposable instance and synthetic Acme Ltd. Create three-item request; issue no-login link; upload dummy PDF; show status/review/flag/reminder draft; unlock/decrypt demo file; export CSV. Explain that demo does not prove production operations, malware scanning, compliance certification or working trial.

## Feedback and paid conversion

Ask what was unclear/slower, first intended use, whether no-login reduces friction, trust evidence needed, current tool replaced, value of encryption, fair/high/unacceptable price, and why they would stop. After two observed workflows, review time, completeness, support and missing features. Present written scope, price, cancellation/data terms and request an explicit paid decision. Do not count polite interest or free use as demand.
