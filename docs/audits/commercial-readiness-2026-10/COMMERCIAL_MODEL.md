Annual gross per customer at £19/29/39/49/59 is £228/348/468/588/708. Three at those prices produce £57/87/117/147/177 MRR. Test £19–£29 initially; £49 needs repeated value evidence.

Planning costs: Hetzner EU CX23 after June 2026 was listed at €6.53/month excluding VAT, before IPv4/backup/add-ons; this is compute only. Object storage provider lists €4.99/month including 1 TB storage/egress, but Tickmark does not currently use it directly. Domain/monitoring estimate £10–£40/month. Storage/backups/email/SMS depend on measured usage. Support at £30/hour: 15 min/customer/month costs £7.50; one hour costs £30. Reserve £150–£500/month averaged for security/legal/accounting/insurance (planning estimate, obtain quotes). Founder-led acquisition has low cash but not zero labour; five hours at £30/hour = £150 CAC/customer.

Assuming £200–£600 fixed monthly operating reserve and ~£43 contribution/customer before support, zero-founder-wage break-even is roughly 5–14 customers. With 15 min support/customer, roughly 6–17; with one hour, roughly 12–34. At 3 customers this does not fund meaningful founder income; 25–50 depends on support/storage; at 100–250 capacity, quota and operations dominate.

At £49, 250 full-year customers imply £147,000 annual sales, above GOV.UK’s current £90,000 VAT registration threshold. Confirm taxable turnover and VAT display/treatment with accountant; taxes and founder take-home are not included. Low-touch solo operation is plausible only if support stays low and quotas/monitoring are real.
