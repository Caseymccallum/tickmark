# First ten conversations and first three paying practices

Initial segment: independent Northern Ireland bookkeepers and sole/small accountancy firms with recurring annual tax/year-end document chasing, under roughly five staff, and no portal used consistently. Exclude firms satisfied with TaxDome, Suralink, SafeSend, SmartVault or an all-in-one suite unless they name a gap. Buyer: owner/partner/practice manager.

Find prospects from public local directories, professional directories and referrals; do not scrape personal contact details or mass-message. Target Downpatrick, Newry, Bangor, Belfast and nearby areas. A qualified conversation establishes recent pain, current alternative/cost, buyer authority, recurring workflow and willingness to evaluate with synthetic data.

Funnel: ten discovery conversations; three actively testing practices; three paying practices. For pilot qualification require repeated pain, no satisfactory current tool, reachable owner, and informed acceptance of bounded evaluation. Paid conversion only after each practice completes two workflows and names measurable time/completeness value.

Stop or reposition if fewer than 3/10 identify frequent meaningful pain, most already solve it in an included suite, none will pilot after demo, no one will pay £19 after experiencing the workflow, or every buyer requires integrations/multi-entity features. Drop-offs may reflect trust in a new solo operator, security procurement, incumbent adoption, setup burden, workflow mismatch or low support capacity.

14-day pilot: day 0 scope/data rules; day 1 synthetic account/key/request; days 2–3 staff test link/upload/review/reminder/export/restore; days 4–10 observe anonymised process; day 11 interview; day 14 close/paid conversion and disposition. Do not use real sensitive files until launch gates pass.

Test £19/month for first 60-day paid pilot, then validate £29 or £49 after usage review. No lifetime discount. Research participants are not prospects until pain, authority, and buying intent are demonstrated.
