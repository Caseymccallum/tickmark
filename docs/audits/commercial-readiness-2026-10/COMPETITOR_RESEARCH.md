| Zendoc | Current site positions law/accounting collection and waitlist; no public price observed | Emerging substitute, maturity/pricing unverified |
| Suralink/SafeSend | Named in repository discovery notes; current price not verified here | Practitioner anecdote highlights entity/return navigation; follow up with real buyers |

“Stop chasing” is category value, not differentiation. Credible narrow wedge: simple outstanding-document checklist, one no-account link and browser encryption for small firms without a satisfactory suite and who value server/operator inability to read file content. Security operations are not yet ready for broad promises. Do not try to match mature incumbents on certifications, support, onboarding, integrations or document management before discovery validates a gap.

Prices are list prices, may vary by region, term, taxes and negotiation. Competitor security statements are their own claims, not independently certified here.
